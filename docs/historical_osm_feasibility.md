Temporal scope remains unsigned under TM-01. Agent2 recommends an explicitly
mixed-period exploratory proxy: 2024 raster patterns with 2026 OSM network and
candidate destinations. Agent2 outputs withdraw actual2024 public-access,
temporal stability/change and current2026 heat-exposure interpretations. Agent3

project's scientific scope on the coordinator's behalf.

Before requesting data, `agent2_historical_pilot.py` defines three3km squares
centred on influential Taman Walang, Segitiga Intirub and Kalijodo candidates;
two timestamps (2024-12-31T23:59:59Z and2026-11-19T11:02:20Z); identical park/highway
predicates and recursive source-node retrieval; and proposed materiality criteria.
Queries are archived with SHA-256. Thresholds of5% availability/threshold changes
or fixed-set top10 Jaccard<.9 are **proposals requiring approval**, not signed
research criteria or retroactive preregistration.

All six actual requests to `https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter` returned
HTTP406 with an nginx rejection body. Exact queries, response bytes/hashes,
timestamps and errors are archived in `outputs/qa/remediation_agent2/historical_pilot/`
and `historical_osm_pilot.csv`. No snapshot, comparable legal topology, historical
route, threshold crossing, or M4 temporal effect was obtained. The failure is an
endpoint/access result, not evidence that historical OSM is generally impossible.

[OSMnx documents historical snapshots](https://osmnx.readthedocs.io/en/stable/getting-started.html)
and [ohsome documents timestamp extraction](https://docs.ohsome.org/ohsome-api/stable/reference/time.html).
A failed query or2026 source revision cannot establish2024/2026 stability. A
historical mapped object also does not establish its physical opening date.

If mixed-period framing is signed and dependent claims are removed, the full
historical requirement can be explicitly deferred. If2024-access claims remain,
this issue is externally blocked pending working historical extraction and
matched AC-01/AC-02 policies. Reuse the archived AOIs/predicates, verify timestamps,
