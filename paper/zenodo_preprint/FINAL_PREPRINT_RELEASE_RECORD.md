## 3. Scientific conventions recorded

- M1–M4 formulas and weights unchanged.
- M4 is an exploratory heat–population–park-deficit screening index.
- Demographic counts are the primary historical model input; shares are
  sensitivity analyses.
- Hybrid population aggregation is the reported baseline; area-fractional
  aggregation is a sensitivity analysis.
- Thermal valid-pixel threshold: 50%.
- Candidate park distance is modelled network access, not verified public access.
- Common sample: 2,536 cells.
- Spatial LISA sample: 2,535 non-island cells.
- Primary LISA: two-sided, 9,999 permutations, Benjamini–Hochberg q = 0.05.
- Primary M4 LISA result: 493 significant cells (252 HH, 227 LL, 7 HL, 7 LH;
  2,042 not significant).
- Alternative LISA conventions remain documented.
- Dynamic World categorical fractions are not used as an empirical result.
- Novelty is rank-change analysis, model-change decomposition and sensitivity,
  not merely combining indicators.
- The manuscript is a non-peer-reviewed preprint.
