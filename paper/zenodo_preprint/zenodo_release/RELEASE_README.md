## Scientific scope

The manuscript is an exploratory spatial screening and sensitivity analysis of
urban heat priorities in Jakarta. It examines how four screening models change
top-decile priorities, attributes the M3-to-M4 change to park addition,
reweighting and age substitution, and reports sensitivity to accessibility,
demographic and spatial-inference assumptions.

It does not claim:

- verified historical heat exposure or personal temperature exposure;
- verified public park access or entrances;
- a validated heat-vulnerability, health-risk or intervention model;
- causal effects of parks, vegetation, age or population;
- that M4 is objectively superior to M1–M3;
- a Jakarta-wide prevalence estimate from the conditional common sample.

## Accepted limitation

Landsat thermal inputs were prepared under a declared 2024 processing
configuration. Retained exports support raster-to-grid reproduction, but the
original Earth Engine scene/task/AOI records are unavailable. Monthly and
seasonal representativeness cannot be established. The authors accept this
restricted interpretation for an exploratory preprint and disclose it in the
manuscript.
