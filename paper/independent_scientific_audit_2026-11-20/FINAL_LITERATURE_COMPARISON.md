systematic review.

Crossref; abstracts and full text were read where legally accessible. Where only
an abstract or publisher preview was available, that limitation is stated.

## Key conclusion

The literature already establishes Jakarta heat mapping, multi-indicator
adaptation prioritisation, composite heat-risk indices, green-space
accessibility, demographic vulnerability and sensitivity of spatial priorities.
The defensible contribution of this paper is narrower: a cell-level attribution
of a sequential screening transition, the empirical redundancy of an age count
with total population, the arithmetic threshold behaviour of the additive park
term, and the propagation of candidate-park/network uncertainty through the
ranking.
